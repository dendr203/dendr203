# Hi, I'm Daniel 👋

**Developer** at [ACRIOS Systems](https://www.acrios.com) and **Cybersecurity master's student** at VŠB – Technical University of Ostrava.
I build low-power IoT devices, from bootloaders and radio stacks to on-device scripting and cryptography. I'm also into machine learning and game AI.

- 🔧 3+ years of professional experience in **embedded C** for battery-powered IoT devices
- 🔐 Master's thesis on **post-quantum cryptography for low-power devices**
- 🖨️ Self-taught **3D modeler**: I design parts in Fusion that fix real-world problems

## 💼 What I do

- **Firmware & bootloaders** in C for STM32 microcontrollers
- **Wireless IoT:** LoRaWAN, NB-IoT and LwM2M
- **Security features** in device firmware, built on applied cryptography
- **Lua scripting API** that customer scripts use on our devices
